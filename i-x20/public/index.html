A fast card game for friends

JUNCTION!

Match four. Call it first. Get your hand down!

3–6 players  •  Best played with quick hands

HOW TO PLAY JUNCTION

1

Everyone gets four cards. The deck contains sets of four identical cards, such as four Moon Mango cards or four Rocket Raccoon cards.

2

Choose one unwanted card. At the same time, every player passes one card to the player on their right and receives one from the player on their left.

3

Keep passing quickly. Your aim is to collect four cards with the same name. Watch what arrives and decide which card to give away next.

4

Call “Junction!” The moment you have four matching cards, press the Junction button and put your hand down.

5

Stack your hands! Everyone else races to put their hand on the Junction caller’s hand. The order decides second, third, and later places.

Tip: Do not reveal which set you are collecting. Passing cards that look useless can confuse the other players.
